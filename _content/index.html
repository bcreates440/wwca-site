What to expect at the show

245 tables, two halls

The Fremont Center and Heritage Hall fill with collector arms, militaria, knives, ammunition, reloading gear, optics and western Americana — laid out across a hard cap of 245 tables so the aisles stay walkable.

Free youth shooting range

Run on-site with the National Wild Turkey Federation. In 2026 it drew 98 young shooters — nearly double what we planned for — every one of them coached one-on-one by certified volunteers. See the youth program.

Raffles worth showing up for

The main gun raffle, a 50/50 cash drawing and a vendor table raffle run all weekend. 2026's winner took home a Weatherby Vanguard Obsidian .270. Raffle details.

Who we are

A family organization, first

The WWCA is, above all, a family-oriented organization. We believe that hunting and shooting sports offer a unique opportunity to instill discipline, ethics, and a profound respect for safety in our youth.

We are a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit run entirely by volunteer members. The Memorial Weekend Gun Show is our one gathering of the whole year and our single fundraiser — which is exactly why we keep table prices reasonable and put the proceeds back into youth education and safety programs.

Tables are capped at 245 for 2027

After an expanded 2026 floor stretched our layout, security and volunteers thin, the board set a hard cap: 245 tables, no exceptions. Everyone — members and non-members alike — must pre-register in advance. There will be no walk-up tables.
