The 2027 table cap

The floor is limited to a maximum of 245 tables, with no exceptions.

In 2026 we expanded across both the Fremont Center and Heritage Hall. It worked for the vendors, but it stretched our layout, our gate security and our volunteer crew past what a handful of people can carry. Last-minute table requests — however much our show chairman wanted to say yes to them — made all three of those problems worse.

Everyone pre-registers. Members and non-members alike. A table reservation form must be submitted in advance, by March 15, 2027. There will be no walk-up tables and no day-of additions.

Once 245 tables are spoken for, we stop. Reserving early is the only way to guarantee both a table and your choice of location on the floor.

Reserve Your Table Join first for member rates

What a table costs

2027 table rates are announced when registration opens. Members always pay less than non-members — typically by about $15 a table — so if you are planning to take a table, joining the association first usually pays for a good part of itself.

The show is the association's only fundraiser of the year, and we deliberately keep table prices reasonable rather than charging what the market would bear.

Before you load the truck

Show rules

  • The 80% rule. At least 80% of your table inventory must be firearms, knives, ammunition, or directly related accessories. This is a collector gun show, and the floor needs to read like one.
  • Pre-registration is mandatory for every vendor, member or not, by March 15, 2027.
  • All federal, state and local firearms law applies on the floor, without exception.
  • Dues must be current to claim the member table rate.
  • Tables are assigned from the reservation list. Reserve early for your choice of spot.
  • Cancellations after the published refund date are not refundable — the date is printed on your reservation form.

Questions about rules, layout or a last-minute problem on show weekend go to the show chairman directly.

Why take a table

What the show does for vendors

Cash on the floor

We put two monitored ATMs directly in the halls — one in the Fremont Center, one in Heritage Hall — so buyers never have to leave the building to find money. In 2026 they moved well north of $20,000 onto the trade tables across 87 transactions.

A buying crowd

Memorial Day weekend in Riverton draws collectors from across Wyoming and from Colorado, Montana and beyond. This is a destination show for people who came specifically to buy, sell and trade.

A table raffle on top

Every table purchased earns an entry into the vendor table raffle drawn during the show — our thank-you to the dealers who make the weekend happen.

We need volunteers for 2027

The WWCA runs entirely on volunteers, and the core crew is stretched thin. If you value this association — or want to get your kids or grandkids involved — these are the jobs we need filled:

Friday evening meal

Setup, serving and cleanup on setup night.

Door & security helpers

Greeting attendees and handing out wristbands at the entrance. A genuinely good job for responsible kids and teens.

Flyer distribution

Posting flyers in your own community, or keeping a stack of WWCA flyers on your table at other regional shows through the year.

Social media & teardown

Keeping our Facebook page shared and active year-round, and group labor for Sunday afternoon pack-up.
