What you get

Why members join

Reduced table rates

Members pay noticeably less per table at the Memorial Weekend Gun Show — historically about $15 a table below the non-member rate. If you take more than a couple of tables, membership pays for itself on the spot.

The Rifle Shot newsletter

Our newsletter carries show planning, board decisions, the statewide Wyoming gun show calendar, promoter contacts and association news. Read past issues.

A vote that counts

Members nominate and elect the board every year. This is a small association where one vote genuinely moves things — the 2026 vice-presidency was a contested three-way race.

The members' dinners

The Friday evening meal and the Saturday catered members and dealers dinner are the social heart of the weekend, and they are for members and vendors.

Youth programs you fund

Your dues underwrite the free youth shooting range and the NWTF JAKES memberships we buy for the kids who shoot it. See where it goes.

A 501(c)(3) behind it

The WWCA is a registered non-profit in good standing. Dues and donations support a genuine charitable mission, not somebody's margin.
