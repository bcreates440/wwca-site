Nurturing the seedlings of tomorrow

The Youth Shooting Range

Free, fully supervised, and open all weekend at the show. In 2026, 98 young shooters stepped onto the line — for many of them, the first time they had ever handled a firearm.

What happens on the line

One kid, one coach, one bullseye

The range is a professionally structured Daisy BB gun setup run inside the Fremont Center Classroom, right off the show floor. Every child who steps up gets one-on-one coaching from a certified safety instructor before they ever touch a trigger. Targets are officially scored.

We planned for up to 50 participants in 2026. Ninety-eight showed up — from across Wyoming, Colorado and Montana. One family brought all seven of their children. One young boy walked off the line beaming with the particular confidence that only comes from hitting what you aimed at.

It is free. It has always been free. It is the clearest expression of what this association is actually for.

A powerhouse partnership

Built with the NWTF

Joan Eisemann

Vice President of the Wyoming National Wild Turkey Federation, lifetime WWCA member, and a Wyoming Game & Fish Hunter Safety Senior Instructor who trains the state's new instructors. She spearheaded the range and brought it to life.

Women in the Outdoors

Certified safety coaches from the local WITO chapter — John Christiansen, Trystan Gorley and Ashton Gorley — worked the line one child at a time, all weekend.

Dubois Cabin Fever NWTF

Sheryl and John Weber of the Dubois Cabin Fever chapter brought the range equipment and the volunteer hours that made 98 shooters possible.

Where the money went. The WWCA funded 73 brand-new NWTF JAKES youth memberships for participants at $10 each — an initial $300 donation followed by a further $183.50, with our local NWTF chapter partners covering the balance. The partnership worked both directions: the Wyoming NWTF doubled its statewide JAKES youth membership off the back of this one weekend.
